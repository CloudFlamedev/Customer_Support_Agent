# Refund Policy

## Eligibility
Customers can request a refund within 30 days of purchase. The item must be unused and in its original packaging. Digital products are refundable within 14 days if they have not been downloaded more than once.

## How refunds are processed
Once a refund is approved, the money is returned to the original payment method. Card refunds take 5 to 7 business days to appear. PayPal refunds take 2 to 3 business days. Shipping fees are not refunded unless the item arrived damaged or the wrong item was sent.

## Non-refundable items
Gift cards, personalized items, and subscriptions already renewed for the current month cannot be refunded. Customers can cancel a subscription at any time to avoid the next renewal.

## Disputes
If a refund request is declined and the customer disagrees, the case must be reviewed by a human support specialist. Agents must not promise an outcome for disputed refunds.
