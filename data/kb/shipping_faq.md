# Shipping FAQ

## Delivery times
Standard shipping takes 4 to 7 business days. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days. Orders placed before 2 PM on a business day are shipped the same day.

## Tracking your order
A tracking number is emailed once the order ships. Tracking updates can take up to 24 hours to appear after the email is received. Tracking is also available under My Orders in your account.

## Shipping costs
Standard shipping is free for orders above 50 USD. Below that, standard shipping costs 4.99 USD. Express shipping costs 12.99 USD for all orders.

## Lost or damaged parcels
If a parcel has not arrived 10 business days after shipping, contact support with the order number. For damaged items, send a photo within 7 days of delivery so a replacement or refund can be arranged.
