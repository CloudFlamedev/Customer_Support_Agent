# Password Reset and Account Access

## Resetting your password
Go to the login page and click "Forgot password". Enter the email linked to your account. A reset link is sent within 5 minutes and stays valid for 60 minutes. Check the spam folder if the email does not arrive.

## Account locked
After 5 failed login attempts, the account is locked for 30 minutes. It unlocks automatically. Resetting the password also unlocks the account immediately.

## Changing your email address
Sign in, open Settings, then Profile, and choose "Change email". A confirmation link is sent to the new address. The change is complete only after the link is clicked.

## Two-factor authentication
If a customer lost their two-factor device, support must verify identity before disabling it. This case must be handled by a human because it is a security-sensitive action.
